Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 22, 2026

QueryShift is an internal automation utility used to create, schedule, and publish content to an authorized Instagram business or creator account. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when QueryShift is connected to Instagram through Meta APIs.

Information We Process

Depending on the permissions granted by the account owner, QueryShift may process limited Instagram account and publishing information, including Instagram account ID, username, profile metadata returned by Meta APIs, access tokens, media URLs, captions, post metadata, publishing status, and API response receipts.

How Information Is Used

Information is used only to authenticate the authorized Instagram account, prepare media containers, publish posts, stories, reels, or carousels, verify publishing status, troubleshoot failed publishing attempts, and maintain an operational record of automation activity.

Data Storage

Access tokens and related configuration are stored in private local project files or secure deployment configuration controlled by the account owner. QueryShift does not sell personal data and does not use Instagram data for unrelated advertising, profiling, or resale.

Sharing

Information may be sent to Meta APIs as required to publish and manage Instagram content. We do not share Instagram data with third parties except service providers or infrastructure required to operate the automation, or where required by law.

Retention

Operational records are kept only as long as needed for publishing, debugging, audit, and account management. Access tokens can be revoked by disconnecting the app through Meta or Instagram account settings.

Data Deletion

To request deletion of data associated with QueryShift, follow the instructions on the Data Deletion Instructions page or email queryshiftofficial@gmail.com.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact queryshiftofficial@gmail.com.
