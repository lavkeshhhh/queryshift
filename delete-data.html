Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: May 22, 2026

You can request deletion of data associated with QueryShift at any time.

How to Request Deletion

Email queryshiftofficial@gmail.com with the subject line "QueryShift Data Deletion Request". Include the Instagram username or account identifier connected to QueryShift so we can locate the relevant records.

What Will Be Deleted

After verifying the request, we will delete locally stored access tokens, Instagram account identifiers, publishing configuration, and operational publishing records associated with the connected account, unless retention is required for security, legal, or abuse-prevention reasons.

Timeline

Deletion requests are normally completed within 30 days after verification.

Revoke Access Directly

You can also revoke QueryShift's access through your Meta or Instagram account settings. Revoking access may immediately stop future publishing automation, but you should still email us if you want locally stored operational records deleted.

Contact

For deletion requests or privacy questions, contact queryshiftofficial@gmail.com.
